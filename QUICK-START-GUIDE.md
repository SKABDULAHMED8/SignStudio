# SignStudio: Quick Guide

SignStudio helps you make a digital signature and fill in PDF files. Your photos, signatures and PDFs stay on your device.

## 1. Make a signature

1. Open **Make a signature**.
2. Choose **Photo or upload** to use a photo, or choose **Draw signature** to write with your finger or mouse.
3. Pick the pen colour.
4. Adjust the picture until the signature looks clear.
5. Choose **Save signature image** to save a copy to your device.
6. Choose **Save to my library** to use the signature again later.

## 2. Find your saved signatures

1. Open **My signatures**.
2. Choose a signature to use it.
3. Download a backup before clearing your browser data. Clearing browser data can remove saved signatures.

## 3. Fill in a PDF

1. Open **Fill a PDF**.
2. Choose **Open PDF** and select your file.
3. Fill in any boxes that you can type into.
4. To add something to a page, choose a tool:
   - **Add a tick**: put a tick in a box.
   - **Add a cross**: put a cross in a box.
   - **Add a date**: choose a date and place it.
   - **Add words**: type your name or other details.
   - **Text in boxes**: put one letter or number in each box.
   - **Add a photo**: choose a photo and place it on the page.
   - **Find boxes**: mark possible boxes on the page. Check the marked areas yourself.
5. Choose **Add your signature**, pick a saved signature and click the page.
6. Drag items to move them. Use **Undo** or **Redo** if needed.
7. Choose **Save finished PDF**.

## 4. Save your details for later

1. Open **My signatures**.
2. Open **Your saved details**.
3. Enter your name, phone number, email and other details.
4. Choose **Save my details**.
5. In the PDF screen, choose your saved details and select **Add my details**.
6. Check every box before saving the PDF.

## 5. Keep your saved items safe

- Your saved signatures and details stay in this browser on this device.
- You can turn on an app password in Settings.
- Download a backup before clearing browser data or moving to another device.
- To open a backup, choose **Open a backup** and enter the backup password.
- SignStudio does not send your PDF or signature photos to a server.

## Important notes

- **Find boxes** looks for box shapes. It does not read printed words or understand form labels. Always check its marks.
- Some PDF files do not have boxes that can be typed into. Use **Add words** or the other tools instead.
- A signature placed in a PDF is a picture of your signature. It is not a certificate-based digital signature.
- Some PDF files use features that SignStudio cannot fill. If a box does not work, add the text by hand.
