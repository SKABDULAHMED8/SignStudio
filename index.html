SignStudio questions

Does SignStudio upload my files?

No. Selected files are processed in your browser. SignStudio does not send documents or images to a SignStudio processing server.

Can the image cleaner remove any photo background?

No. It is designed to isolate handwriting or ink from plain paper. It is not an AI object-selection or general photo background removal tool.

Can I add a handwritten or cryptographic signature to a PDF?

You can draw a handwritten signature and place it on a PDF. SignStudio does not apply cryptographic digital certificates or verify signer identity.

Where are the results saved?

You download the result to your device. SignStudio does not keep a document library or processing history.
